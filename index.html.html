Core Principles

🌙 Evening RuleEvery non-Wednesday evening = 30–45 min mandatory. Wednesday = completely free. Exam day evenings = rest. This is the non-negotiable structure.
⚡ Speed-Through RuleEvery block is a ceiling not a target. Finish early, move on. Makes revision feel like a game not a grind. Mock session 30 May is the one exception.
Normal Monday structureP1+P2 Maths · P3+P4 History lesson · P5 FREE = Christianity · P6 RS lesson · Evening = Phil or Ethics rotation.
Wednesday is goldenUp to 150 min in-school revision (P2 FREE + 100min DS). Evening completely free as a reward. 3–4 subjects covered every Wednesday.
2–3 subjects every school dayNo single-subject days. Christianity, History, Phil and Ethics all weave together from Week 1.
Phil & Ethics from Week 1Philosophy starts Thu 7 May. Ethics starts Mon 4 May. Not left to the last minute — built progressively every week.
Post-exam pivotAfter every exam, the very next free slot switches to the next subject. Don't dwell. Keep momentum.
UCL requires A + B minimumChristianity (3/10) and Italy (6/10) are your highest-risk exams. Both receive the heaviest focus throughout this plan.
